Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to hello@ohyeti.app. A real person reads every message.

I bought Remove Ads but I see ads again

Open the game while online: it checks your purchase with the App Store or Google Play on launch. If ads are still there, tap Restore Purchases under Remove Ads on the main menu, using the same account you bought it with.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Your best distance, settings and achievements are saved on your phone, so deleting the game removes them. Remove Ads is tied to your store account and comes back after reinstalling.

Can I turn off sound or vibration?

Yes: the Music, Sound and Buzz buttons at the bottom of the main menu.
